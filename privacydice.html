Privacy Policy for Premium Dice

Effective date: April 27, 2026

Premium Dice is developed by JJMT Production ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account and we do not directly collect personal information such as name, email address, or contact details.

However, the app uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Advertising ID (AAID)
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • App interactions and usage data
  • Diagnostics and crash data

2. Third-Party Services

Premium Dice uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – for rewarded ads and interstitial ads
  • Google Play Billing – for in-app purchases

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

3. Advertising

Premium Dice displays advertisements via Google AdMob, including rewarded ads and interstitial ads.

Rewarded ads are optional and provide in-app benefits when viewed. Interstitial ads may appear at natural transition points in the app.

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent choices, location, and device settings.

4. In-App Purchases

The app offers optional purchases through Google Play Billing (for example, removing ads or unlocking premium features).

All payments are processed securely by Google. We do not collect or store payment information.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process personal data based on:

  • Consent – for personalized advertising and data processing via AdMob

6. Consent and GDPR

In regions where required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the app will request your consent before showing personalized ads or using certain data.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the app’s privacy options.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to request deletion of your data
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to restrict or object to processing
  • Right to data portability

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Data Retention

We do not store personal data ourselves. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their own policies.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may process data for advertising and analytics purposes.

10. International Data Transfers

Third-party services used in the app may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States.

These transfers are handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Children’s Privacy

Premium Dice is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Data Security

We do not directly store personal data. Third-party providers implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data.

13. Do Not Sell / Share Information

We do not sell personal data. However, advertising partners may use data for ad personalization in accordance with their policies.
