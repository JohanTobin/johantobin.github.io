Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications developed and published by Johan Tobin, including but not limited to Movie Night Roulette and Countdown Timer.

The developer respects user privacy and is committed to transparency regarding how data may be handled. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how users can contact the developer regarding privacy-related questions.

1. Information Collection

The developer does not require users to create an account or provide personal contact information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers in order to use the apps, unless explicitly stated inside a specific app.

However, some apps may use third-party services that automatically collect certain technical data in order to function properly, improve stability, prevent abuse, or display advertisements.

Depending on the app and enabled features, these services may collect:

  • Advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device information (such as device model, operating system, app version, language, and general device settings)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Diagnostics data, including crash logs and performance information
  • Ad interaction data, such as ad views, impressions, and clicks
  • Basic app usage data provided by third-party SDKs for analytics or service functionality
Note: Not all data types listed above are collected by every app. Data collection depends on the specific app, its features, and the third-party services integrated into it.

2. Advertising

Some apps may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob.

These services may automatically collect certain information to provide, personalize, measure, and protect advertising services, in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

More information about how Google handles data can be found here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Analytics, Diagnostics, and App Functionality

Some apps may use third-party services such as analytics, crash reporting, or diagnostics tools to:

  • Understand app performance
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Improve stability and user experience
  • Detect fraud, abuse, or technical issues

If such tools are used, they may process technical information such as crash logs, device identifiers, and app interaction events.

4. How Information Is Used

Information collected by the apps or their integrated third-party services may be used for:

  • Providing app functionality
  • Displaying advertisements
  • Measuring ad performance
  • Improving app performance, reliability, and stability
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, or misuse of services
  • Complying with legal obligations

5. Third-Party Services

The developer’s apps may use third-party services, depending on the specific app and version, including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase services, such as Analytics or Crashlytics

These third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users’ personal information.

Data may be processed or shared only as necessary through integrated third-party service providers that support app functionality, analytics, diagnostics, advertising, security, or legal compliance.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data retention depends on the specific app, the enabled features, and the third-party services used. Some technical or advertising-related data may be processed directly by third-party providers under their own retention policies.

If you have a privacy-related request, including a request to delete data associated with a specific app where applicable, you may contact the developer using the contact details below.

8. Children’s Privacy

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Where required, apps intended for broader audiences will handle advertising and data processing in accordance with applicable child privacy requirements and platform policies.

9. Users in the EEA, UK, and Similar Regions

Users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other regions with similar privacy protections may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data, where applicable.

To exercise such rights, please contact the developer using the contact information below.
